## [1.2.6] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- **Reel audio mixer:** when you add music to a reel, set the volume of the original sound and the music separately before you post.
- **30 days to restore a deleted account:** a deleted account is hidden, not erased, for 30 days. Sign in again in that time and the app offers to restore it. A page deleted on its own can be restored from Settings.
- **Announcements from The Hotel Media** now appear in your Notifications, with a title and message.
- **Café / QSR plan** (₹2,999 a month) for cafés, quick-service restaurants, bakeries and dessert and juice shops: menu and online food delivery.

### Changed

- The Restaurants / Nightclubs / Bars plan is now ₹6,999 a month.

### Fixed

- Bug fixes and performance improvements.
