# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes go into the latest version on the App Store and Google Play. Please update before reporting.

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| 1.2.x (latest) | Yes |
| Anything older | No |

The apps run on iOS 15 or later and Android 7.0 or later.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security problems in public issues or discussions.**

Report them privately, either:

- by email to **support@thehotelmedia.com** with "Security" in the subject, or
- through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/soensingh/the-hotel-media/security), if it's shown.

Helpful details:

- the app version and whether it's iOS or Android, with the OS version;
- what you found and what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- whether you've told anyone else.

Please use only your own accounts while testing. Don't access, change or delete other people's data, and don't make real payments or bookings against businesses you don't own.

## What happens next

- You'll get a reply within a few days confirming the report was received.
- We'll keep you posted while we look into it and work on a fix.
- Once a fix ships, we're happy to credit you in the release notes, if you'd like.

Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.

## Scope

In scope: The Hotel Media apps for iOS and Android, the website at https://thehotelmedia.com, profile share links, the business web console at https://property.thehotelmedia.com, and the handling of sign-in, bookings, food orders, event tickets and payments.

Out of scope: spam, social engineering, denial-of-service attacks, automated scanner output without a working impact, and problems in third-party services we don't control (such as Razorpay, Firebase, the App Store and Google Play).

There's no paid bug bounty at the moment.
